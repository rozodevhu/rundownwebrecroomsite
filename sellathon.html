Ink Inc. February Sellathon

The Ink Inc. Sellathon has returned! This time, we’re rewarding 250,000 tokens for every 1,000,000 tokens in gross sales of Premium Inventions during February. That’s right! You sell a lot of stuff, you get a lot of tokens, then we give you even more tokens! And yes, you can then take those tokens and cash them out for real money*.

*For more information, check out Partnered Creator F.A.Q.

 

RULES

1. Bonuses are determined by gross sales (sales before the 30% marketplace fee) of Premium Inventions during the month of February

2. Sellers must adhere to the Code of Conduct; They must not be banned during the Sellathon

3. You need RR+ to receive any bonuses

 
 

HOW TO PARTICIPATE

Just sell any Dorm Skins and/or Premium Inventions during the month of February. Take advantage of programs that promote your work—like Invention Tower, Featured Dorm Skins, and Featured Inventions (submit via the Ink Inc. Discord)—and use social media like YouTube and Instagram to bring in other potential buyers.

Take this opportunity to figure out what a winning business model looks like to you. Connect with players that view your inventions. Find out what they like and see if you’d like making it too. And since the winners are based on gross sales (not the number of owners), experiment with the price to get the most out of each purchase. Learn more about pricing on the Ink Inc. Discord.

 
 

Get Connected

Stay informed! Get exclusive announcements on Community Commerce, find out how to get your work featured, and connect with community and staff by joining the Ink Inc. Discord. Find us in-game at ^InkInc
