GAME JAM - AUGUST 2022

Let’s make some games! If you’ve wanted to try making a game for the first time, experiment with a new technique, or just build something that will delight you, it’s a great time to try!  This isn’t a competition - no prizes, no pressure. We just want our community to have some fun building things for people to play!

The Game Jam theme is Rootin’ Tootin’ Shootin’! Build a game and tag it with #gamejam-rts. That’s it!

We’ll have some get-togethers at the beginning and end of the game jam to meet up with other builders, swap circuit tips, bounce ideas off of each other, and show off our games at the end.

The details:

  • This Game Jam runs August 28th - September 4th, 2022. While the deadline is not strict, we’ll be featuring some of our favorites from those that are public by 11:59 pm (PST) on September 4th!

  • Games should have a defined beginning and end and accommodate at least 2 people (preferably more). Examples of rooms: ^Showdown,^MadMaxFuryRoadPVP, and ^EdgeOfUnknown.

  • You can use pre-existing assets from your previous builds, but we encourage you to try something new! Rooms should be started on or after August 28th, 2022.

  • Tag your room with #gamejam-rts to enter it into the game jam!

  • We must be able to find a connection to the theme in your room. Make an action-packed shooting game (does not have to be a PvP)!

  • Game jam events:

    • Kick-offs: Early and Late on August 28th, 2022

    • Wrap-Up: September 4th

FAQ:

  • How many people can we have in our group?
    As many as you want and can manage!

  • Is there a prize?No, but we will have a small in-game memento of the Game Jam for all participants if the room fits the theme!


Need Help?

Join the Rec Room Discord Server and check out the #building, #circuits, #circuits-v1-legacy, or #game-jam channels to meet other creators, see what everyone is working on, and get tips on getting started!

You can also find a lot of answered questions and helpful community members on the Rec Room Subreddit.

Check out the 'Custom Rooms Tools and Tutorials' article in our help center! 

If you still have questions, need technical support, or run into bugs, please check out our Help Center.
